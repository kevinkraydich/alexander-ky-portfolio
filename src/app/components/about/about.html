About Me

I'm a medical student at Michigan State University College of Human Medicine, pursuing an M.D. with a Public Health Certificate. My clinical interests center on anesthesiology, perioperative care, and emergency medicine, with electives in musculoskeletal medicine, microbiology, and lifestyle medicine.

Before medical school I studied biochemistry at the University of Michigan, with a minor in applied statistics, and spent four years in the O'Rourke Lab studying adipose tissue biology. I've continued that work alongside new projects in anesthesiology, emergency medicine, and community health — most recently as a 2026 Medical Student Anesthesia Research Fellow at the University of Nebraska.

Outside the hospital I row, cook, and teach — coaching novice rowers in Grand Rapids, running suture and chemistry workshops for students, and serving on the MSU-CHM Continuous Quality Improvement Committee.
