About Me

Hello, I’m Alex! I’m a fourth year medical student at Michigan State University - College of Human Medicine, pursuing an M.D. with a Public Health Certificate. I grew up in Holland, MI and went to the University of Michigan for my undergraduate studies in Biochemistry and Applied Statistics.

Growing up with Cambodian refugee parents, I often helped my family navigate medical appointments, bridging language and cultural barriers that first inspired me to pursue medicine. I spent my clinical years of medical school in Flint, MI where I worked with patients from diverse backgrounds and gained a deeper understanding of how social and economic barriers affect access to care. My experiences in Flint and time volunteering in Cambodia strengthened my interest in health equity and global health.

I also have a passion for research and medical education. Ultimately, I hope to pursue a career in academic anesthesiology where I can combine patient care with research, teaching, and opportunities to work internationally. Outside the hospital, I enjoy cooking, rowing, and mentoring.
