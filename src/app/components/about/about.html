About Me

I am a fourth-year medical student currently applying to residency programs, with clinical interests in internal medicine, patient-centered care, and evidence-based practice. My training has given me the opportunity to work alongside physicians across multiple specialties and in diverse clinical settings.

Alongside my clinical rotations, I pursue translational research focused on improving outcomes for underserved patient populations. I am passionate about mentorship, medical education, and bridging the gap between bedside care and clinical investigation.
